    How to use Constellations

    Select a star to open its details, or select its name to rename it. Drag a star to move it. Press Ctrl+Z or ⌘Z to undo a resolution. Right-click a star, then select the star that must finish first. Right-click empty sky to add a star and press Enter to save it. Click empty sky or press Escape to cancel.
